# Espresso, as configured on this machine

## Grinder

- **Comandante C40**, burrs cleaned last month
- Dose **18.5g** in, 18g out, so about 0.5g retained

## Recipe

- 18g in, **36g out**, which is 1:2
- **28 seconds** total, starting the timer at the first drop
- Water at **93°C**, not 94. 94 tastes burnt on this bean

## Beans

Currently the **Ethiopia Guji**, two weeks off roast. It wants 1:2.2 and a slightly cooler
93. When it is gone, the next bag is the Colombia Huila and it wants the original 1:2.

## What is wrong with it

- Grinder retention is high enough that the first shot of the day is always rubbish
- The tamper has a wobble that only shows on a light roast
- Temperature is not repeatable to better than about 1°C, which is worse than it sounds
