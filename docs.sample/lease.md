# Flat lease — 14B Mercer Street

## Parties

Landlord: Ferndale Properties Ltd. Tenant: Kiran Jain. Lease starts 1 July 2025.

## Term

The lease runs for **11 months**, ending **31 May 2026**, with no break clause in the first six.

## Rent

**£1,450 per month**, due on the 1st, 5 days late. Council tax included. Utilities not included.

## Deposit

**£1,450**, held by the landlord. Returned within 14 days of leaving, less fair wear and tear.

## Maintenance

The landlord covers structural repairs and the boiler. The tenant covers anything below
£100 and anything caused by negligence. **No decorating without written permission.**

## Notice

Either party may terminate after the first six months by giving **60 days written notice**.
Notice given by email counts if sent to the address in the header.

## Clause 14 — the one that matters

*Clause 14.2: the tenant may terminate early on serious health grounds, with one month's
notice and supporting evidence from a clinician. No penalty is payable.*

Clause 14.2 is the break most people miss. It is one month, not sixty days.
