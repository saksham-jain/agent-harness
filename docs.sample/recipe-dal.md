# Dal, the one that actually works

## Ingredients

- 250g **toor dal**, soaked four hours
- 1 tsp ghee, 1 whole cumin seed, 4 dried red chilli
- 400g tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp **Sendha salt** — not table salt, it behaves completely differently

## Method

1. Boil the dal with a whole tomato until it collapses, about 40 minutes.
2. Fry the cumin and chilli in ghee until the cumin stops being white.
3. Add the chopped tomato, cook down to a paste — this is the part everyone skips.
4. Mash the dal back in with the fried tomato. Do not add water.
5. Salt to taste at the end, never earlier.

## Why the last step matters

Salt early and the dal skins before it cooks, so it goes grainy. Salting at the end keeps
it soft. The whole dish depends on a five-second decision.
