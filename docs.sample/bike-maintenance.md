# Bike maintenance

## The Ironworks

**07700 900733**, closed Mondays. They do while-you-wait and they are honest about what
needs doing, which is rarer than it should be.

## Intervals

- Chain oil every **200 km**, or after any rain ride
- Gear and brake cables: check monthly, replace annually
- Tyres to the line marked on the sidewall, not the tread. Mixing makes it square.

## Current state

Rear tyre replaced **2 October**, 28mm. The old one had a cut that was not visible from the
side and would have gone at about 6,000km.

Front brake pads at **60%**. Rear at 45%. Neither is urgent.

## The chain that squeaks

New chain fitted **August 2026**, and it squeaked for a fortnight. Cassette was fine. It
was the chain being installed slightly out of line, which the shop corrected without being
asked.
